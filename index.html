About JK Roofs Craft

Built by craftsmen, not a catalogue counter

JK Roofs Craft runs its own roll-forming line in Coimbatore — every sheet is cut to your exact length, not the nearest standard size.

We stock galvanized (GI) and colour-coated (PPGI) steel in a full range of gauges, for everything from a home extension to a 40,000 sq ft industrial shed.

Our coatings are chosen for South India's climate: extreme heat, coastal humidity, and heavy monsoon rains. That's the job the sheet has to do before it does anything else.
